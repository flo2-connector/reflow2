A project brain.

One shared design across all your repositories: what it must do, what you decided and why, and what you found along the way. Kept in a graph any AI agent can read, and updated as you work.

Set it up Source on GitHub

The example below is made up: the Gearheads, a school robotics team with a handful of repositories, a season of competitions, and students who graduate.

Where the knowledge lives

Scattered notes leave when people graduate. A project brain keeps them, linked.

What goes into the brain

Small, typed facts, linked to each other and to the code.

One brain across all your repositories

Each repository keeps its own graph. The hub links them and holds no copies: a record can point into another repository's design, a change is followed from one repository into the next, and a requirement is handed to the repository that owns it. Access follows the repository.
